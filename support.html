How often should I use the mask?
Three to five evenings a week is the rhythm most people sustain, with a fixed ten-minute cycle each time. Consistency beats marathon sessions — see The Science.
Can I use it with retinol or acids?
Use the mask on clean, dry skin first, then apply your actives afterwards. If you’re on photosensitising medication or under dermatological treatment, check with your doctor first. (Placeholder guidance — have a professional review before launch.)
Is it waterproof? Can I rinse it?
The mask isn’t waterproof — wipe the shell with a barely-damp cloth and let it air dry. Never submerge it or rinse under a tap.
