Wavelength 02 · 830nm

Near-infrared,
backstage.

830nm is just past what your eyes can see — you’ll feel it as gentle warmth rather than brightness. It travels deeper than red light, backing it up from underneath. In the Pro, both wavelengths fire from the same dual-chip diodes, so you’re never choosing between them.

Used for: the deeper half of the routine — comfort, recovery feel, staying power.

Dose, not endurance

Why exactly ten minutes?

Light follows a dose curve: more isn’t better, it’s just more. Ten minutes at our irradiance lands in the useful zone for a home routine — long enough to matter, short enough to repeat. The mask times it for you and switches off, so the dose can’t creep.

Honest answers

Science questions.

Is it safe for home use?
LED photobiomodulation at consumer intensities is widely used at home; LUMASK adds eye-area light blocking and an automatic stop. If you’re pregnant, photosensitive, or prescribed light-sensitive medication, check with your doctor first. (Placeholder disclaimer — have your regulatory review rewrite this.)
How soon will I see anything?
Honest answer: it’s cumulative, not instant. Most placeholder testimonials above describe 6–12 weeks of consistent use. If a device promises overnight transformation, it’s selling, not measuring.
Can I use it with my serums?
Yes — cleanse first, use the mask, then apply your serums afterwards so nothing sits between the light and your skin during the session.
Every day, or every other day?
Three to five times a week is the rhythm most people sustain. More isn’t better with light — consistency is.
