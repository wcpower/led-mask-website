How it started

A drawer full of
almosts.

Every gadget made big promises and asked for more than we had: thirty-minute sessions, apps, chargers, guilt. The masks that survived were the ones that asked for ten minutes and nothing else — so we obsessed over making those ten minutes as good as the science allows, and then getting out of the way.

We publish every spec because “proprietary blend” is not a specification. We ship one button because your skincare shouldn’t need a login. And we price the way we’d want to be priced — no fake list prices, no permanent sales.

What we optimise for

Four promises.

01

Honest specs

Wavelengths, irradiance, weight, battery — published, not implied. If a number is an estimate, we say so.

02

Ten minutes or nothing

We cut every feature that made the ritual longer. If it doesn’t fit ten minutes, it doesn’t ship.

03

Designed to be used

Soft shell, one button, auto-stop. Comfort and friction decide adoption — not spec sheets.

04

Support that answers

Real replies within a day, two-year warranty, thirty-day returns with no interrogation.
